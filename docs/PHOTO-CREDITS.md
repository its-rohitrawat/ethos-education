# Photo credits

Written for: the Ethos team and whoever maintains the site's imagery.

The site's provisional photographs come from **Unsplash**. They are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), which allows free commercial use with no permission needed. Attribution is not required but is appreciated, so the photographers are listed here.

They are **placeholders for atmosphere**, not photographs of Ethos students, staff or offices. The `provisional: true` flag on each image records this in the code. Before launch:

1. Replace them with Ethos's own photography where possible. Real counselling sessions, offices and (with consent) students build far more trust than stock.
2. Keep any stock images that remain from implying something untrue. No caption or alt text should suggest that the people shown are Ethos students or that a pictured campus is a partner.
